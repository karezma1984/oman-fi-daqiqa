Write a short script for a student presentation. Aim for around 100–130 words for one minute, depending on speaking speed.

A simple 60-second structure

  1. Opening: Say hello and introduce your topic.
  2. Two key facts: Explain what it is and why it matters.
  3. National connection: Link it to Omani identity, values or heritage.
  4. Closing: End with one memorable sentence.
Tip: Read your script aloud and time yourself. Keep sentences short and use facts you can verify.

Presentation routine
